Reference board · 22 October 2026

Spatial RF tools worth borrowing from

01 / Hamina Planner

The closest interaction reference: its official demo shows placing access points and updating results. Borrow direct manipulation and continuous feedback. Its floor-plan propagation model is much broader than this Friis draft.

Open website and interactive examples ↗

Official Hamina demo, streamed from Hamina. Press play; no local copy is downloaded.

02 / UISP Design Center

Useful for a canvas that dominates the workspace, compact tools, and explicit link creation. For this project, adopt the spatial hierarchy on a metre grid.

Explore live map planner ↗

Screenshot of UISP guest welcome, captured during this research. It demonstrates the workspace hierarchy, before placing a link.

03 / CloudRF

The official UI guide documents placing a transmitter and receiver visually or entering coordinates, then inspecting a path profile. Borrow the dual visual/precise workflow. Terrain and Fresnel analysis are outside this draft.

View UI samples and path-profile documentation ↗
